Reporting obligations under § 32 BSIG (24 hours / 72 hours / 1 month) Guide, chapter 4, incident response plan (04) and incident reporting template (05). An internal record of the required details, not an official BSI form. Included
Management duties under § 38 BSIG (implementation, oversight, training) Guide, chapter 5.1, training log (09, including management), an approval field in the policies, and a prompt for an internal memo (prompt pack, P02). No dedicated board or oversight template. Partial

AI Act Art. 50 — Transparency / internal AI use policy AI use policy (03); guidance on Art. 50 in the guide (chapter 7.4), in the AI system inventory and as a raw draft in the prompt pack (P08). No finished, legally reviewed transparency notice. Included
AI system inventory AI system inventory (10), with fields covering, among other things, role, Art. 5 and Art. 50. Included

Questions

FAQ

What is NIS2?

The NIS2 Directive is the EU cybersecurity framework. In Germany it has been implemented by the NIS2UmsuCG; the duties sit mainly in the BSI Act (BSIG), which entered into force on 6 December 2025. The kit helps you understand and implement the most important steps.

Who is the kit suitable for?

For small and medium-sized companies in Germany that want a structured way to address duties under the BSIG (the NIS2 implementation) and the EU AI Act. The kit offers a starting point; individual legal or compliance advice may still be required.

How do I get access?

After purchase you receive an email with the link to download a ZIP file (the guide as a PDF, the templates as DOCX and XLSX). Access is available immediately.

Is this legal advice?

No. The kit is a practical aid for orientation and preparation. It does not replace individual legal or compliance advice.
